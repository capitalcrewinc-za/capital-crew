Frequently asked questions.

Can I make a contribution here?

You can use the bank details shown after selecting an amount. Transfers must be made through your own banking app.

Are the daily amounts guaranteed?

No. Any daily amounts shown are not guaranteed and must be confirmed against the official written terms.

Can I register or sign in?

Account access is not available on this website.

Where can I get verified information?

Contact the organisation through independently verified official channels and request current legal terms, fees and risk disclosures.
