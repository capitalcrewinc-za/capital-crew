SUPPORT

Help centre.

Answers for members and a clear escalation path.

How do I create a Capital Crew account?

Enter your full name, email address and password, then submit the registration form. A one-time confirmation code must be sent to the email address you entered. Your account is only activated after the correct code is confirmed. If the code expires, request a new one.

Why do I need a confirmation code?

The confirmation code helps confirm that you control the email address used for your account. Capital Crew should require this confirmation when a new account is created and again whenever a member signs in. Never share a confirmation code with another person.

What should I do if I do not receive my code?

Check your spam or junk folder and make sure the email address is correct. You can request a new code. If you still need help, use the WhatsApp or phone support details in About & Contact and tell Briana that you are contacting the team from the Capital Crew website.

How do I make a contribution?

First complete identity verification. Once your account is approved, choose a plan, review the contribution and fee, select an authorised payment method and continue to the configured payment provider. A payment is only credited after the provider confirms it to the server.

How does account verification work?

Open Profile & KYC, complete your profile and submit the requested identity document. Your status can be pending, verified or rejected. Deposits remain disabled until verification is approved.

Where can I see my receipt?

Your live system should generate a server-side payment confirmation and receipt after the payment provider confirms success.

What if I forget my password?

Use the production password-reset flow configured by the site owner. A secure reset link or one-time code should be sent to the verified email address. Never ask support to send or disclose your password.

When can I withdraw?

Withdrawals become eligible after 10 full days of investment, counted from the date your investment is confirmed. After an eligible withdrawal is approved, it normally takes 1–3 working days to reflect, depending on the payment method and provider. Identity checks, compliance reviews, payment reversals or provider delays may affect timing.

How do I contact support?

Open About & Contact and use WhatsApp Briana, the support phone number, or the support email. The WhatsApp message opens with a note that you are contacting the team from the Capital Crew website and need help.

4. Contributions and payments

Members select an available contribution level and are shown the contribution, applicable fee and total before payment. Payments are processed through an authorised payment provider. A payment is not treated as successfully settled until the provider confirms it to the Capital Crew server.

4. Daily-share / return information

Any daily amounts shown on the Money List must reflect the actual approved product terms. They must not be described as guaranteed returns unless the underlying product is legally structured to provide such a guarantee. All risk, performance, liquidity and withdrawal conditions must be disclosed before a member commits funds.

5. Wallet and account balance

Member balances shown in the dashboard are calculated from server-side ledger entries created from verified events such as confirmed payments, approved allocations and authorised withdrawals. The browser cannot create or increase a balance by itself. Amounts shown as available for withdrawal remain subject to the 10-day investment period and the final product rules.

6. Withdrawals

Unless the final approved product terms state otherwise, a member may request a withdrawal only after 10 full days of investment, measured from the date the investment is confirmed by Capital Crew. Once an eligible withdrawal has been approved and released for settlement, Capital Crew targets 1–3 working days for the funds to reflect, depending on the selected payment method and the relevant payment provider or financial institution. The actual timing may be longer where identity verification, compliance checks, weekends/public holidays, payment reversals, bank processing or provider outages apply. All withdrawal requests, approvals, rejections, fees and settlement events must be recorded server-side.

7. Refunds and payment disputes

Payment disputes, reversals, chargebacks and refunds are handled according to the payment provider's rules and Capital Crew's final refund policy. The transaction ledger should record every state change.

8. Privacy and security

Personal information must be processed according to the applicable privacy laws and the final Capital Crew privacy notice. Never store card numbers, CVVs, banking passwords or payment-provider secrets in the website code.

9. Prohibited use

Members may not use the service for fraud, money laundering, unauthorised payments, impersonation, account sharing or other unlawful activity.

10. Changes

Capital Crew may update platform policies and terms subject to the notice and consent requirements that apply to the final service.
