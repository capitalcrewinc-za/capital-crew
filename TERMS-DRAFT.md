# Capital Crew Terms & Conditions — draft template

**This document is a template for legal review, not legal advice.**

The final terms must be prepared for the actual Capital Crew entity, product, investment/community structure, payment providers, jurisdiction and regulatory obligations.

Key sections to finalise:
1. Eligibility and account access
2. Identity/KYC and AML checks
3. Contributions and payment processing
4. Money List / daily-share terms and risk disclosures
5. How member balances are calculated
6. Allocation/earning rules
7. Withdrawals and settlement times — eligibility after 10 full days of investment; target settlement 1–3 working days after approval, depending on payment method/provider
8. Refunds, chargebacks and disputes
9. Fees
10. Privacy and POPIA compliance
11. Security and account responsibility
12. Suspension/termination
13. Complaints and dispute resolution
14. Changes to terms
15. Governing law and jurisdiction
16. Contact details and registered business information

Do not publish example daily-share figures as guaranteed returns unless the underlying product is legally structured and documented to support that statement.
