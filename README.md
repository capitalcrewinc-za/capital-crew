# Capital Crew Member Centre

A responsive static website with a money list, selectable amount cards, automatically populated bank-transfer details, member guidance and support information.

## Payment information
- Bank: GoTyme Bank
- Account holder: Emma Jacobs
- Account number: 51042322891

Selecting an amount fills the payment panel and creates a suggested payment reference. Transfers are made independently through the user's banking provider. The website does not receive, process, or verify payments.

## Important
The displayed daily amounts are not guaranteed returns. Confirm the current written terms, risks, fees, and legitimacy of any payment request independently before transferring money.

## Run locally
Extract the ZIP and open `index.html` in a browser. Upload the extracted folder contents to your static hosting provider to publish.
